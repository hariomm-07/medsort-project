AI-based medical waste detection and segregation

Four colour-coded bins following Indian biomedical waste categories, with a low-power VLSI controller that gates its clock when idle.

Why this project

The problem

Hospital waste is often mixed. One needle in the wrong bag can injure a worker, and infectious waste spoils recyclable material.

The approach

A camera image is classified by an AI model, and a small controller opens the matching bin among four colour-coded bins.

The saving

The controller's clock is switched off while no item is being processed, which reduces needless switching activity.

FAQ

Is the classifier real?

Yes when waste_model.pt is trained and loaded: the Flask API classifies image pixels. Without it, classification is unavailable and the page says so. Predictions below the confidence threshold need manual verification.

Is any hardware connected?

No. Sorting is simulated on screen.

Are the power savings measured?

No. The bars count simulated clock toggles; use your synthesis tool's power report for real numbers.
